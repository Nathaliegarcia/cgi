## Philosophy

Rank Math uses a **data-driven, AI-powered approach** to SEO. Instead of giving generic recommendations, it understands search intent, supports multiple focus keywords, identifies semantic variations, and suggests improvements that matter for rankings.

**Important caveat from Rank Math themselves:** The SEO score "is a guide and not to be seen as a target." It is "there only for your reference and doesn't affect your actual rankings. Treat it only as a to-do list, nothing more." A post with a lower score can rank well if other factors (competition, domain authority, relevance) are optimized.

## SEO Scoring System

### Score Scale
Rank Math scores content on a **100-point scale** across 21 real-time tests.

### Score Color Codes

| Score | Color | Meaning |
|-------|-------|---------|
| 81-100 | Green | Well-optimized post |
| 51-80 | Yellow | Room for improvement |
| 0-50 | Red | Poorly optimized, not following basic SEO practices |

### What the Score Measures
- Focus keyword placement (title, slug, first paragraph, H2, conclusion)
- Meta description optimization
- URL slug quality
- Content length and depth
- Readability (short sentences, bullet points, transition words)
- Internal and external links
- Schema markup implementation
- Media optimization (images with alt text, compressed sizes)

## The 21 SEO Tests

### Basic SEO Tests

| # | Test | What It Checks | How to Pass |
|---|------|----------------|-------------|
| 1 | **Focus Keyword in SEO Title** | Keyword appears in the meta title | Include exact keyword in title |
| 2 | **Focus Keyword at Beginning of Title** | Keyword appears at the start of the title | Lead the title with your keyword |
| 3 | **Focus Keyword in Meta Description** | Keyword appears in the meta description | Include keyword naturally in description |
| 4 | **Focus Keyword in URL** | Keyword appears in the page slug | Use keyword as the URL slug |
| 5 | **Focus Keyword in First 10%** | Keyword appears in the opening portion of content | Mention keyword in the first paragraph/10% of text |
| 6 | **Focus Keyword in Content** | Keyword appears throughout the body | Use keyword naturally through the content |
| 7 | **Content Length** | Word count meets minimum threshold | Over 600 words minimum; 2,500+ recommended for top scores |
| 8 | **Focus Keyword in Subheading** | Keyword appears in H2/H3 headings | Include keyword in at least one subheading |

### Additional SEO Tests

| # | Test | What It Checks | How to Pass |
|---|------|----------------|-------------|
| 9 | **Focus Keyword Density** | Keyword frequency in content | Appropriate density (not too sparse, not stuffing) |
| 10 | **Meta Description Length** | Character count of meta description | Under 160 characters, optimized and keyword-rich |
| 11 | **Title Readability** | Title uses power/emotion words | Include positive or negative sentiment words (e.g., "best", "ultimate", "proven") |
| 12 | **Title Has Number** | Title contains a numeral | Include a number in the title where applicable |
| 13 | **Table of Contents** | Long content has a ToC | Add a table of contents for posts over 1,500 words |
| 14 | **Use of Media** | Images/videos present in content | Minimum 1 image; 4+ images/videos for full score |
| 15 | **Image Alt Text** | Images have alt attributes with keywords | Add descriptive alt text with relevant keywords |
| 16 | **URL Length** | Slug is short and clean | Keep URL to 3-5 words, use hyphens, remove stop words |
| 17 | **Internal Links** | Links to other site pages present | Include relevant internal links |
| 18 | **External Links** | Links to outside sites present | Include relevant outbound links |
| 19 | **Do-Follow External Links** | At least one external link is followed | Ensure not all external links are nofollow |
| 20 | **Keyword in Image Alt** | Focus keyword appears in at least one image alt | Use exact keyword in one image alt attribute |
| 21 | **Short Paragraphs** | Paragraphs are readable length | Keep paragraphs short and scannable |

### Key Thresholds

**Content Length Scoring:**
| Word Count | Effect |
|-----------|--------|
| < 600 | Fails the test |
| 600-1,500 | Partial score |
| 1,500-2,500 | Good score |
| 2,500+ | Maximum score |

**Media Requirements:**
| Images/Videos | Score |
|--------------|-------|
| 0 | Fails |
| 1 | Partial score |
| 2-3 | Good score |
| 4+ | Full score (100%) |

## Content Best Practices

### According to Rank Math's Guidelines

1. **Keyword Research First** - Identify target keywords before writing
2. **Search Intent Matching** - Understand what users expect (informational, transactional, navigational)
3. **Comprehensive Coverage** - Aim for 2,500+ words to cover topics thoroughly
4. **Structured Content** - Use H2/H3 headings, lists, tables, and ToC
5. **Media Rich** - Include 4+ relevant images/videos
6. **Internal Linking** - Connect related content on your site
7. **External Authority** - Link to authoritative external sources
8. **Readability** - Short paragraphs, clear language, transition words
9. **Unique Title** - Use power words and numbers to improve CTR
10. **Optimized URL** - Short, keyword-rich, hyphenated slugs

### Title Optimization Tips
- Include the focus keyword at the **beginning** of the title
- Use **sentiment words** (positive: "best", "amazing"; negative: "worst", "avoid")
- Include a **number** where applicable ("7 Tips", "2026 Guide")
- Keep titles compelling and click-worthy while being accurate

### Meta Description Best Practices
- Under **160 characters**
- Include the **focus keyword** naturally
- Write a compelling summary that encourages clicks
- Include a **call to action** where appropriate
